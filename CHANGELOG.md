# Changelog

- Added Modeled rendering with adjustable wavelength, material, thermal, transmission, radar, and hypothetical source assumptions across all 14 bands.
- Added editable material regions, a material map, separate display palettes, and local reference-image comparison.

- Retained the approved Spectrum Simulations logo, creator attribution, email, privacy, and static/offline builds.
