# Observation library provenance

Checked October 3, 2026. These are published telescope observations, not generated images. The app keeps the publisher's framing, orientation, color mapping, and embedded labels. Source PNGs (1920 × 1080) were resized to 1280 × 720 and encoded as WebP at quality 88. No reconstruction, recoloring, or geometric registration was applied by Spectrum. The slider compares outreach renditions; it does not claim pixel-level radiometric equivalence or simultaneous exposures.

## Reuse basis and attribution

The SVS pages link to [NASA Images and Media Usage Guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). Those guidelines allow educational/informational use, including computer simulations and web pages, with attribution and without implying endorsement. Selected source pages provide the image credits above and do not mark these selected assets as third-party copyright exceptions. Chandra's [image-use policy](https://chandra.harvard.edu/photo/image_use.html) likewise permits NASA/SAO material with acknowledgement and distinguishes non-NASA components.

These credits remain visibly associated with each pair and link to its source. NASA/ESA/STScI/Chandra/Spitzer do not endorse Spectrum. The assets are separate from the application's MIT license; forks must preserve credits and comply with the providers' terms. No agency logos are bundled. NASA imagery is used only in the educational comparison library, not the promotional sharing image. The sharing image derives from the project's existing approved Facebook artwork.

## Scientific content

Lesson explanations are short paraphrases based on the linked source pages. Color legends describe published display mappings, not literal invisible-light colors. No numerical accuracy score, temperature measurement, or hidden-feature reconstruction is claimed. Source pages remain available for instrument and observation context.
